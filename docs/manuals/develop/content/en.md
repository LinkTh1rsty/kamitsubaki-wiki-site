Associate content through stable entity IDs and follow src/content/README.md and the implemented metadata rules. Chinese, Japanese and English sources retain the same entity identity; existing generators handle Traditional Chinese. Run pnpm validate:content, pnpm docs:check and the build/link checks after changes. Reader and contributor instructions remain in their respective books. Never copy a whole content snapshot over another task.
