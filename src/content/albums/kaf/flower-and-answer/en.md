## Overview

Kafu 2nd EP "Hana to Kaitou" (Flowers and Answers).

"Hana to Kaitou" is an EP featuring 3 tracks: "Answer," "Keshiki," and "Abunormal."

"Answer" serves as the 11th ending theme for the TV anime Black Clover, while "Abunormal" is a collaboration track with the IMMERSIVE SONG PROJECT for the 
ultra-immersive energy drink "ZONe." All songs were written, composed, and arranged by Vocaloid producer Kanzakiiori. Following her next release "Tomadoi 
Telepathy," this EP is packed with Kafu's new appeal, ranging from ballads to upbeat rock tunes.

The package was released in 2 formats — the Original Edition featuring illustrations by PALOW. was exclusively available at the FINDME STORE,
while the Black Clover Edition features newly commissioned artwork depicting a collaboration between Asta, the protagonist of the TV anime Black Clover, and Kafu, 
and was released exclusively at Animate stores nationwide.
