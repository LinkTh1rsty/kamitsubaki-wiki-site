## Overview

Azsagawa is a singer and singer-songwriter affiliated with SINSEKAI RECORD under KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO. Known for his powerful, expressive, and rock-infused vocal style, he actively performs across both physical and virtual musical landscapes.

## Career Highlights

- Began official activities in September 2022.
- Released notable singles and albums including "Shifter", "Black out", and "Kaihen -Tsumi-".
- Held major live concerts including his 2nd One-Man Live "PORT_A" at Zepp Shinjuku.
