## Overview

BEMA (琶舞) is a virtual singer who debuted on April 30, 2025. With the September 5, 2026 renewal of [KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio), the in-studio labels were consolidated into PHENOMENON RECORD, which now represents her (the [SINSEKAI RECORD](/en/projects/archive/sinsekai-studio) name was retired).

Official descriptions note that she depicts shadowed musical worlds with a crystalline voice that blends into electronic sound. Her activities center on digital singles and cover lives, often releasing alongside fellow studio artists such as ASU.

## Activity Briefs

- **April 30, 2025**: Debuted as a virtual singer.
- **May 6, 2025**: Released the single "[Symbiotic Dominion](/en/songs/bema/originals/symbiotic-dominion)".
- **October 31, 2025**: Held her first free full cover live, "Dark Night LIVE" (YouTube Live).
- **November 4, 2025**: Released the single "[Hoshokusha](/en/songs/bema/originals/捕食者)".
- **December 24, 2025**: Released the single "[First Aid](/en/songs/bema/originals/fuaasutoeido)".
- **December 25, 2025**: Released the Cover Live Album "[Dark Night LIVE (Cover Live)](/en/albums/bema/Dark-Night-LIVE-Cover-Live-1858175130)".
- **November 2–3, 2025**: Appeared in KAMITSUBAKI WARS 2025 Korakuen Front.
- **March 3, 2026**: Released the single "[Tawamure](/en/songs/bema/originals/戯re)".
- **May 5, 2026**: Simultaneous release with ASU, "[Tsukiyo ni Mariage](/en/songs/bema/originals/月夜nimariaaju)".
- **July 8, 2026**: Simultaneous release with ASU, "[Alt Skin](/en/songs/bema/originals/alt-skin)".
- **September 5–6, 2026**: Appeared in KAMITSUBAKI WARS 2026 Yokohama Front.
