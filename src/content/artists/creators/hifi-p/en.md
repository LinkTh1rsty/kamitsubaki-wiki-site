HiFi-P is a sound creator active on YouTube and TikTok, known for Hi-Fi tracks and memorable melodies. His work combines refined sound design with catchy tunefulness, earning broad recognition across online music communities.

He produces and releases music under the "[Musical Isotope](/en/artists/solo/musical-isotope)" brand and provides songs for various artists. Notable works include "Ryuusenkei Mayday" (流水線メーデー) for [KAF](/en/artists/vwp/kaf) x [KAFU](/en/artists/isotopes/kafu), and "Imperfect" for [ASU](/en/artists/solo/asu). These collaborations demonstrate his versatility and ability to craft compelling music across different styles.

HiFi-P is affiliated with [KYOKAI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kyokai-studio) (transferred from [KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO](/en/projects/labels/kamitsubaki-studio) in September 2026) and was formerly a member of KAMITSUBAKI's CREATOR FARM, serving as an integral member of the studio's music creation team.
