## Overview

Onuma Parsley is a composer, singer-songwriter, and Vocaloid producer affiliated with ANARCHIC RECORD under KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO.

Known for seamlessly blending EDM, Future Bass, City Pop, and Hip-Hop with addictive synth arrangements, he has produced viral hits such as "Corruption" and "Egoist". Within KAMITSUBAKI, he frequently composes and arranges tracks for artists like KOKO and Harusaruhi.

## Career

- Began producing Vocaloid music in 2017.
- Joined KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO on its founding day, October 18, 2019.
- Released his best album "ave" in 2020 and launched his career as a self-vocal singer-songwriter.
