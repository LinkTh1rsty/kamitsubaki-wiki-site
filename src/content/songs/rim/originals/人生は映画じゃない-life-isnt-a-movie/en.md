"人生は映画じゃない" is track 5 on the collaborative album *[imagine](/en/albums/rim/imagine)* by Guiano and [RIM](/en/artists/vwp/rim), released on September 20, 2023. The official release page credits all lyrics, music, and arrangement to Guiano; guitar on this track is by Shoma Ito.
