Hello, observers.

This update focuses on supporter benefits in your account, clearer follow-up conversations after submitting content, and everyday reading refinements across the site.

## 01 · My support and benefits

[My Space](/en/account/) now includes a "My support & benefits" section. Linked supporters can check their active tier and validity period, read earned updates, review voting records and beta applications, and manage notification preferences for project updates, submission reviews, and expiry reminders. Earned posts, voting history, and awarded beta access remain available after natural expiry.

The [support page](/en/support/) streamlines its main Afdian flow. Supporter profile submissions now verify contact emails directly in-page while preserving your inputs throughout the request.

## 02 · Review discussions and resubmissions

The new [My reviews](/en/contribute/review/) page—also linked from the [Creator Center](/en/account/creator/) and the [Contribute hub](/en/contribute/)—brings together submission status, revision history, and reviewer feedback.

Authors can reply to feedback threads to add context, and when changes are requested, revise and submit a new round while keeping earlier revisions and comments intact for comparison.

## 03 · Workspace headers and reading details

Workspace pages now share consistent back links, eyebrows, and serif section headings. [Observer Projects](/en/community/activities/) detail pages place key event facts and participation links above the full description, and the contribution hub now summarizes submission steps for both timeline events and community projects.

Synchronized lyrics on song pages also add full keyboard controls, alongside localized source labels in the AI terminal.

## 04 · Entry maintenance and community edits

Legacy editor submission formatting has been reconciled without unnecessary markup churn, incorporating community additions and copy edits for [KAFU](/en/database/isotopes/kafu/), [KAMITSUBAKI CITY UNDER CONSTRUCTION](/en/database/projects/kamitsubaki-city/), and [DUSTCELL](/en/database/artists/groups/dustcell/).

Thank you to everyone who contributes records, shares feedback, and supports the observatory.
