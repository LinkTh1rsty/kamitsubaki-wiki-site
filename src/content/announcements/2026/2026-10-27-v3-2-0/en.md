Hello, observers.

**V3.2.0 introduces [Visual Roam](/visuals/), an independent space built around visual browsing.** Alongside structured reference lookups, it offers a quieter place to experience KAMITSUBAKI through imagery.

## 01 · Visual Roam: another way to see KAMITSUBAKI

Enter from the animated filmstrip portal on [Explore](/en/explore/#visual-roam) or visit [Visual Roam](/visuals/) directly.

Complementing the structured catalog and contribution tools in the [Gallery](/en/gallery/), Visual Roam opens directly onto a masonry wall of published photos. Select any image to launch the high-resolution viewer with smooth zoom, swipe navigation, creator attribution, and source links.

## 02 · Curated albums, search, and direct links

Switch from the full photo stream to [Albums](/visuals/albums) to browse collections organized by artist and visual category, or narrow the wall using text search, tags, and sort options.

Individual photos and albums keep clean, shareable URLs that resolve directly to the selected item even when it sits deeper in the catalog.

## 03 · Continuous masonry and gallery workflow

The masonry layout loads upcoming windows continuously as you scroll while keeping card positions steady across filtering and album navigation, without manual pagination breaks.

Visual Roam reflects approved public works from the wiki catalog. Uploading new photos, building concept sets, and suggesting classifications continue in the [Gallery](/en/gallery/) and [Photo Submissions](/en/gallery/manage/).

Thank you to everyone contributing artwork records, attributions, and classifications.
