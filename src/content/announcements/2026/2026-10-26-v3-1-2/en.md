Hello, observers.

This update introduces the Observer Dashboard on the homepage to bring updates and everyday tools together, alongside refinements to event discovery, posters, and cross-timezone schedules.

## 01 · Homepage Observer Dashboard

The [homepage](/en/) now features the Observer Dashboard, gathering upcoming events, site notices, the embedded AI assistant, and quick shortcuts into one workspace.

From the overview tab, you can switch between official events and community projects or catch up on recent announcements. Without leaving the page, you can also open the AI assistant, search the wiki, switch light and dark themes, or jump straight to [Account](/en/account/), the [Gallery](/en/gallery/), and [Contribute](/en/contribute/) across desktop and mobile viewports.

## 02 · Activity Center, full posters, and local times

The [Activity Center](/en/events/) and [Observer Projects](/en/community/activities/) unify browsing, filtering, calendar subscriptions, and sharing. Browse the next 30 days, upcoming deadlines, or the past archive—including events that ended earlier today—and copy share text or export share cards in one click.

Event posters adapt to their aspect ratio with full-poster zoom and artwork credit inspection, while mobile layouts keep primary participation actions above tall posters. Cross-midnight and international events retain the organizer's date while showing your local time in agenda lists, with cleaner row alignment on mobile screens.

## 03 · Reader, search, and loading interactions

Entry sidebars keep sticky positioning with softer scrollbars, search inputs refine placeholder contrast, control height, and keyboard navigation, and image previews and filter controls now share consistent loading feedback.

Thank you to everyone organizing projects, contributing event details, and helping refine the reading experience.
