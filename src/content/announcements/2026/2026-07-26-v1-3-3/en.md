summary: "V1.3.3 introduces site-wide search, available from the navigation or with Command／Control + K. Results can be filtered by artist, song, album, project, or observation log. The search engine tolerates Latin spelling errors and short fuzzy queries, matches across Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese shinjitai forms, and prioritizes exact titles and highly relevant entities. The interface now includes arrow-key selection, Enter to open, Esc to close, focus trapping, a full-screen mobile layout, a custom text-entry cursor, and complete opening and closing motion. The AI index has moved to a structured V2 schema with aliases, summaries, heading hierarchy, and collection statistics. Lightweight front-end indexes are loaded separately for the active language, avoiding a download of the complete trilingual AI corpus while improving retrieval quality and parsing cost. This release also fixes search viewport boundaries, result links, scroll locking, and the uncovered strip on the right edge of artist hover backgrounds."
