Hello, observers.

**V3.3.0 introduces shared speech blocks and dialogue import tools for interviews and conversations, alongside a refreshed article reading layout and smoother submission workflows.**

## 01 · Interview speech blocks and quick dialogue import

[Entry editing](/en/contribute/editor/) and [article submissions](/en/articles/submit/) now share speech blocks tailored for interviews, translated talks, and program transcripts. Desktop screens present a clean speaker sidebar while mobile screens place avatars and names above each passage. Speakers can link to existing wiki person entries—inheriting their link and avatar—or use custom avatars and accent colors, and editing a speaker updates every matching turn in the document.

The workbench also includes a quick dialogue import dialog. Paste `Name: text` transcripts or upload a Markdown file to detect speakers and turns while keeping unmarked lines as regular paragraphs. Map speakers to existing roles, preview the formatted conversation, and insert it in one undoable step. Downloadable templates and copyable conversion prompts for external tools are included without sending your writing to any AI service automatically.

## 02 · Refreshed article list and reading layout

The [Articles](/en/articles/) directory and reader adopt a refined layout across desktop and mobile screens, improving typography, table of contents navigation, and related-entry cards. Published articles now display public author avatars, credits, origin badges, and category labels, with clearer empty-filter and error states.

## 03 · Submission state sync and YAML date compatibility

The [Creator Center](/en/account/creator/) and [My reviews](/en/contribute/review/) keep deletion states and publication progress in sync, preserve unsent reply drafts, isolate local state across account switches, and add contextual help tips along the submission flow.

Across export and submission paths, the editing workbench also normalizes ambiguous YAML dates and full ISO timestamps while leaving unambiguous scalar fields such as track durations untouched.

## 04 · New tobia entry and community copy edits

A new entry for singer-songwriter [tobia (跳亜)](/en/database/artists/solo/local-486b0763-fb4c-4598-a4b0-670297287b00/) is now live and indexed in the [Gallery](/en/gallery/) entity directory, alongside community corrections to entry dates and prose.

Thank you to everyone translating interviews, writing articles, refining entries, and supporting the observatory.
