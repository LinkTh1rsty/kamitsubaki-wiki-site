## Introduction

KAMITSUBAKI CREATION is the creative division and production network within KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO. Bringing together composers, illustrators, visual designers, and video directors, it provides continuous aesthetic and technical support for musical releases, music videos, live performances, and worldbuilding projects.

## Core Functions

- Music production, arrangement, and sound design
- Character design, key visuals, and illustration
- Music video direction and live concert visual production
