## Introduction

ANARCHIC RECORD is an experimental music label in the KAMITSUBAKI ecosystem dedicated to cutting-edge sound, independent creators, and alternative expressions. It has featured distinct producers and singer-songwriters such as Onuma Parsley and Guiano.
