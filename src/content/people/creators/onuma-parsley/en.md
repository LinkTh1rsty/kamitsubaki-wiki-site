## Overview

**Onuma Parsley** is a founding producer of KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO signed to ANARCHIC RECORD. Best known for viral hits like "Egoist" (over 10M views) and "Corruption", he writes across electronic, City Pop, and alternative pop genres.

## Detailed Chronology and Personal Preferences

**Chronology (detailed)**

| Date | Event |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2017-06-29 | Uploaded the debut track “Yoake Mae” |
| 2018-02-20 | Uploaded “Corruption”, from which he began to attract wide attention |
| 2018-03-07 | Uploaded “After School” sung by fellow producer Ame nochi Ame, later releasing a Hatsune Miku Vocaloid version |
| 2018-04-24 | Began uploading self-covers of his own songs |
| 2018-09-12 | Uploaded “Egoist”, which drew an immediate and large response |
| 2019-06-20 | Stopped posting to niconico after “Billy”; all later work was uploaded to YouTube only |
| 2019-10-18 | Announced his participation on Twitter alongside the founding of KAMITSUBAKI STUDIO |
| 2020-02 | Paused his activity as a Vocaloid producer with the release of the album *ave* |
| 2020-09 | Released the single “Latency”, returning to public view as a singer-songwriter |
| 2021-05-08 | Uploaded “Minamizuka”, sung by the virtual singer [KAFU](/en/database/isotopes/kafu) |
| 2023-05-10 | Uploaded “Bonnie & Clyde”, sung by the virtual singer [COKO](/en/database/isotopes/coko) |

**Personal preferences and community notes**

- **A long collaboration with an illustrator**: early song art was mostly drawn from public works by various artists on Piapro, while later art has mostly been handled by **Honda Souichi**; the two are close and call each other by nicknames.
- **Contact with producers of his generation**: he is close to the producer **Kakashi**, the two have remixed each other's songs and interact often on Twitter.
- **A personal alt account**: he runs a Twitter alt named **Numamitaina** (meaning “like a swamp”) for everyday trivia, and has uploaded two covers on YouTube under that name.
- **In-house writing at KAMITSUBAKI**: within the studio he frequently writes for [KOKO](/en/database/artists/solo/koko).
- **Stated preferences**: he has tweeted that he likes BL ({{spoiler::self-described as a fudanshi}}); his favourite utaite are WaMi, Rei and Miyashita Yuu.
- **Musical background**: he loved singing from childhood and went to karaoke often; he began learning an instrument properly in the second year of middle school, played in a band in high school, and performed the band's originals at a live house.

> **Style summary**: Onuma Parsley's trajectory is unusual even among KAMITSUBAKI creators — from an early phase of “Piapro artwork plus gentle songwriting”, through large-scale deletions and a stylistic rebuild, to a settled identity of “electronically charged poison pop”, and then a turn to self-performed singer-songwriter work after pausing his producer activity. That curve is itself a sample of KAMITSUBAKI's model in which creators are free to keep transforming.
